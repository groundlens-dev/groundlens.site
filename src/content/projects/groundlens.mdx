*groundlens* is a Python library that triages LLM outputs by grounding signal — deterministic, sub-second, no second LLM in the loop.

Two methods, zero LLM dependencies:

- **SGI** (Semantic Grounding Index) — measures whether a response engages its source material. For RAG systems with context.
- **DGI** (Directional Grounding Index) — measures whether a response's displacement in embedding space aligns with grounded patterns. Context-free.

```bash
pip install groundlens
```

```python
from groundlens import compute_sgi

result = compute_sgi(
    question="What is the capital of France?",
    context="France is in Western Europe. Its capital is Paris.",
    response="The capital of France is Paris.",
)
# result.flagged → False
```

Reported skill, with controls. On confabulations that stay in register, every embedding-similarity method, this one included, declines toward chance: classic encoders reach 0.62 to 0.68, raw cosine 0.595. Under an authorship-matched control, decoders that appeared to score 0.93+ fall to 0.66 to 0.68. The high 0.6s is the ceiling of the class, not a groundlens shortfall. Domain calibration sharpens the easy end and leaves the blind spot intact (0.626 to 0.689). Use it to triage, then escalate what it cannot settle to an entailment check.
